Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Daily City Planner is an iOS puzzle game provided by Tomás Ruiz-López. This policy explains how the app handles information and the choices available to you.

Game progress and device features

Your puzzle progress, streaks, tutorial status and preferences are stored on your device. The app does not require an account. Notifications and Live Activities use Apple's device features. Sharing a result happens only when you choose to share it through the iOS share sheet.

The app checks Apple's App Store service for available updates. Network services receive connection information, such as an IP address, when your device contacts them.

Advertising and privacy choices

The free version uses Google AdMob to display interstitial ads when you submit a completed board for review. Google may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, device or app identifiers, ads viewed, interactions, crash logs and performance information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and maintain its services.

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to check whether consent is required before requesting ads. Where available, select Ad Privacy Options on the home screen to review or change your choices. You can continue playing when an ad is unavailable. An active no-ads purchase disables ad requests and presentations.

The current app does not request Apple's tracking permission or access the advertising identifier through that permission. Privacy choices do not necessarily prevent all processing needed to deliver contextual ads or maintain the service. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps.

Purchases

Apple processes in-app payments. RevenueCat helps validate purchases, restore them and determine whether your no-ads entitlement is active. This involves purchase and subscription records and an app user identifier generated by the purchase service. We do not receive your payment card details. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

Analytics and crash diagnostics

Starting with version 1.1, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand app usage and Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose crashes and improve reliability. Analytics processes an app-instance identifier, app and device information, approximate location derived from masked IP addresses, screen views, sessions and other app interactions. It can automatically measure purchase and subscription events, including the product and price, associated with that app-instance identifier.

Crashlytics processes crash stack traces, app and operating-system versions, device information and diagnostic data. Analytics may supply breadcrumbs describing events before a crash. We do not add names, email addresses, account identifiers, puzzle layouts or purchase receipts to these reports. Puzzle progress and streaks remain stored on your device.

Firebase Analytics advertising-identifier and vendor-identifier collection, advertising personalization, advertising data sharing and ad-network registration are disabled in this app. Analytics and crash reporting are separate from ad delivery and remain enabled when a no-ads purchase is active. See Privacy and Security in Firebase and Google's Privacy Policy.

Storage and service providers

Local progress remains on your device until it is removed, such as when you delete the app; device backups may retain copies. Service providers retain information under their own policies and may process it outside your country. We use these providers to operate purchases, advertising, analytics, crash diagnostics and platform features described above.

Your choices and requests

You can manage ad privacy choices in the app when that option is required, control notifications in iOS Settings, and manage subscriptions through your Apple account. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information or object to its processing. Contact us to make a request. Information held by Apple, Google or RevenueCat may also require a request through the relevant provider.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact trlmobilesoftware@gmail.com. We may update this policy when the app or its services change and will update the date above.
